04 / From concept to operation

The engine moved into live work.

I built a working pipeline for campaign preparation: contacts moved through research and routing, personalized sequences were loaded in HubSpot, and outreach reached active campaigns. The hardest work happened before a message was sent: finding a relevant reason, checking the claim, and preparing a usable record for the broker.

Research
AI research and confidence checks helped distinguish a specific, sourced trigger from a thin lead.
Routing
Low-confidence records took a market-segment route instead of receiving invented personalization.
Review
The broker retained control of suitability, relationship context, and enrollment.
05 / Selected work

Three threads. One principle.

Across outbound, publishing, and handover, I used AI to make the team’s expertise easier to apply. Open a project to see the work behind it.

PROJECT 01 / BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

CRE Outbound Engine

Structured research, relevant outreach, and responsible AI checks.
+
THE SYSTEM / FROM SIGNAL TO REVIEW

Designed a research-first system to help prepare more personalized outreach at scale while keeping a person responsible for approving and sending messages.

QualifyResearchRouteDraftVerifyStage in CRMHuman review

The system combined prospect qualification, source-backed research, routing, draft preparation, checks, and CRM staging. Low-confidence research followed a more general segment route instead of fabricated personalization. Review and enrollment remained human decisions.

PROJECT 02 / THOUGHT LEADERSHIP

Beyond CRE Content Engine

Turning broker expertise and market developments into educational content.
+
THE EDITORIAL LOOP / RESEARCH TO PUBLISHING

Helped develop a repeatable editorial process for Beyond CRE, the brokers' educational LinkedIn presence. The work connected timely research to a consistent voice and a publishing queue.

Find a topicCheck sourcesDraft in voiceTrack in AirtableDesign or scriptHuman publish
  • Start with a timely topic and check the underlying source before turning it into a teaching point.
  • Use a shared Claude project for audience, voice, and format guidance, then draft a post for human editing.
  • Track ideas and status in Airtable; prepare Canva AI design prompts or video scripts when useful.
  • Sarah and the team decide what is accurate, useful, on voice, and ready to publish.

Since my co-op, the team has adapted the workflow to its own publishing rhythm and continues posting AI-assisted research.

PROJECT 03 / HANDOVER

Documentation & Handover

Making the outbound workflow understandable and repeatable.
+

I documented the outbound workflow, its research and routing logic, the checks before enrollment, and the points where a person had to decide. That work made the system easier to inspect, troubleshoot, and hand over.

Writing down the decisions mattered as much as writing the automation.

06 / What I carried forward

The co-op ended.
The opportunity didn't.

I arrived knowing how to experiment with tools, and left with a much better understanding of which problems were actually worth solving. That's the part I keep taking into new rooms.

During ENCOR

Learned commercial context directly from Sarah, Rob, and the team through conversations, real projects, client priorities, and constructive feedback.

After ENCOR

I carried that way of working into subsequent projects bringing practical AI solutions to commercial real estate: begin with the people who know the business, then build around the work they actually do.

A note of gratitude
